# Figure 4 k–l legend

**k, Age-associated changes across mouse brain regions.** The percentage of cells with high age scores is shown for oligodendrocytes and microglia in CC/ACO, striatum (STR) and cortex (CTX). Small translucent dots represent individual mice. Larger points and connecting lines indicate age-group means for each region; these are group-level trajectories, not longitudinal measurements of the same animals. Young, middle-aged and older groups contain 4, 7 and 9 mice, respectively. High-scoring cells exceed lineage- and region-specific young-reference thresholds.

**l, Regional age-associated changes.** The heatmap summarizes descriptive full-cell older-minus-young changes in percentage points (ODC, oligodendrocytes), without additional pairwise significance tests. Percentage points denote absolute differences between percentages, not relative percentage changes. The combined scatterplot shows the within-mouse CC/ACO–CTX difference for each lineage; smaller dots represent individual mice, larger points and lines indicate group means, and the dashed line indicates zero. Older-minus-young changes in the regional contrast were 24.9 percentage points for oligodendrocytes and 23.9 percentage points for microglia. Two-sided full-pipeline age-label permutation tests on fixed balanced cell subsamples yielded exploratory BH q values of 0.0020 and 0.0024, respectively (9,999 permutations; two comparisons). Displayed effect sizes use all cells, whereas permutation tests use matched observed and null subsamples. Mouse age directions and young-reference thresholds were refitted during permutation testing, conditional on the fixed joint representation (Methods).

## 中文图注

**k｜不同脑区的年龄相关变化。** 小点为每只小鼠在一个脑区的高年龄得分细胞比例，大点和连接线为同一脑区三个年龄组的组均值。连接线不是同一只小鼠的纵向轨迹。年轻、中年、老年分别有 4、7、9 只小鼠；每只小鼠在三个脑区各贡献一个观察，统计单位始终是小鼠。颜色表示脑区：CC/ACO 红、STR 金、CTX 蓝。

**l｜区域年龄相关变化。** 左侧热图为全细胞老年−年轻比例变化的描述性汇总，不能解释为各脑区之间均已进行显著性检验。右侧合并展示两个谱系的同鼠 CC/ACO−CTX 比例差，蓝色表示少突胶质细胞，橙色表示小胶质细胞。小点为个体，大点和线为年龄组均值，虚线为零。脑区比例差的老年−年轻变化分别为 24.9、23.9 个百分点；固定均衡子样本上的 9,999 次双侧全流程年龄标签置换，两项探索性 BH q 分别为 0.0020、0.0024。全细胞效应与子样本检验是不同观测条件下的估计，不能混为同一统计量。

“Cells with high age scores”是基于表示和年轻参考阈值的操作性定义，不等于已验证的衰老细胞。算法、置换和统计限制详见 Methods。
